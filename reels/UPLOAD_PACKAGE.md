## How to upload

### Instagram Reels
1. **+** → **Reel** → choose the MP4.
2. Tap **Next** without adding music. The audio is already mixed and royalty-free.
3. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose the matching cover JPG. Check the profile-grid preview.
4. Paste the **Instagram caption** below.
5. **Tag people / Add topics**: add the topics listed for each reel.
6. **Advanced settings** → turn on **Add AI label** for Reels 2 and 5 (they contain AI-generated video). Optional for the others.
7. Keep **Also share to Facebook** on if you use Facebook.
8. **Share**. Straight after posting, add the **pinned comment** and pin it (long-press → Pin).

### YouTube Shorts
1. YouTube app → **+** → **Upload a video** → choose the MP4 (it's vertical and under 60s, so it becomes a Short automatically).
2. **Title**: paste the YouTube title. **Description**: paste the YouTube description.
3. **Altered or synthetic content** (Details → Show more): choose **Yes** for Reels 2 and 5, **No** for the others.
4. **Related video** (Reel 1 only): link your full video "Is REA Worth the Hype? I Tested It on a Kanban App". This puts a tap-through link on the Short.
5. Audience: **No, it's not made for kids** → **Upload**.
6. Thumbnail: Shorts use a frame you pick in the app (YouTube Studio app → Edit → Thumbnail). Choose a frame where your face and the on-screen title are visible.

### TikTok (optional)
1. **+** → **Upload** → choose the MP4 → **Next**.
2. Paste the **TikTok caption**. Cover: **Select cover** and pick a strong frame.
3. **More options** → turn on **AI-generated content** for Reels 2 and 5.
4. **Post**.
