## Where the delivery and the intent disagree (flag to Kalyan)

1. **The reveal reads flat.** "Paperclip AI" is the name the viewer must remember, but it's one of the quietest beats (peak −22.6). A name card is justified here. No hype effect, because the voice doesn't support hype.
2. **The CTA is rushed.** At about 245 WPM it's the fastest part of the take, and the quietest. The caption must carry "comment COMPANY" so it can be read even if it isn't heard.
3. **"rework their work"** sounds like it was meant to be **"review their work"**. Captions will show what was actually said unless Kalyan approves showing "review". Re-recording that one line would fix it properly.
4. **"right?"** at 11.8 s is very quiet and adds nothing. The plan drops it, which saves 0.3 s.
5. **"break into task"** was said for "break it into tasks". This is a minor slip and the captions show it as spoken.

Honest limit: these are measurements and taste rules. I can't hear the feel of the take the way a viewer does.
